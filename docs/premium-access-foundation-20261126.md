Tracks #65. Source baseline 04da5d7bf6f55c4659d2aed1898694ff497c9f64. Approved price: EUR 6.99/month and EUR 69/year, one Premium product; Free remains free. No product IDs, fiscal code or fiscal treatment have been confirmed. A tax-inclusive consumer price is proposed, not yet authorized as a fiscal setting. The owner reports sending the inquiry to Xolo. No need to repeat it. Only a live Stripe connection is currently available. No account-wide changes, payments, provider activation, database migration, public UI or production deployment in this draft.

This first isolated server-side increment validates the exact catalog and makes a bounded access decision only from a validated catalog, authenticated owner UUID, server-owned Stripe account/customer/subscription binding and normalized authoritative payment evidence. Its fixture IDs and fiscal code are deliberately synthetic and must never be configured in a real account. Raw inputs from the browser, email matches, metadata, payment-success redirects and unverified webhooks are not evidence.

Contract: catalog must match a freshly retrieved product and two prices in the intended authenticated Stripe account and environment. Only the two approved EUR amounts, flat recurring intervals and quantities, explicit tax behavior, matching product/tax code, and app-specific proposed lookup keys qualify. Adapter must retrieve the account and product/price objects with the same restricted client. No trusted catalog is created from shape-only config.

Proposed conservative policy for internal testing: active paid subscription, no paused collection, exact current paid coverage, no known or unresolved risk, no trial or grace, no access at or beyond expiry or effective cancellation. Evidence freshness is capped at five minutes, and each returned grant is valid only until the earliest evidence expiry, period end or effective cancellation. This is a draft policy, not a final customer cancellation/refund promise. The actual server authorization boundary must check a current private DB projection and enforce revocation atomically; a positive result here cannot by itself authenticate a payment, verify a Stripe invoice, prove risk clearance, or protect an API. A future adapter must reconcile invoice lines, actual payments, refunds, disputes and fraud signals against the exact subscription item and account. Invalid, stale and unknown data fail closed; public error results contain no Stripe identifiers.

Scope: three new server-only modules, one regression suite, this contract and generated source integrity files through existing CI. No dependency or runtime wiring change. The next increment is private transactional subscription persistence and the authentic Stripe reconciliation adapter; Checkout, signed idempotent webhooks, scoped Customer Portal and per-provider entitlement enforcement must follow, with full integration tests including disconnect after expiry. Do not merge or enable this draft merely because fixture tests pass. Do not expose paid Checkout until tax, real provider pilot and security gates are confirmed. No user data or secrets in fixtures.
