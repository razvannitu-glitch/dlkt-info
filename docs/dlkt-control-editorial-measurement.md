# DLKT Control: editorial measurement design

Status: decision record, not an activated data source. Initial release improves discoverability of the existing voluntary measurement choice, and derives the descriptive repeat-browser share from existing opt-in snapshots. No new tracking, cookie, identity linkage, retention extension or billing change is authorized by this release.

## Editorial intelligence: release gates

Existing audience consent covers page entries, recent activity, coarse geography and traffic sources. It does NOT silently extend to reading history, impressions, card selection, saves, shares or category interactions. A separately explained voluntary choice is required before collecting new interaction data; old consent must not migrate automatically. Reading and account use must remain unrestricted. Keep existing opt-in off by default, respect GPC/DNT and provide withdrawal.

Start with public-reader category-level aggregates, not personal interest profiles. Reuse the canonical editorial taxonomy only after review for sensitive categories. Measure a qualified impression (at least half a card visible continuously for one second while document is visible) and explicit open action for that same impression. Deduplicate rerenders and retries, exclude prefetch/background activity, keep actual opening distinct from impression and from outbound source click. A category's open rate is openings attributable to qualified impressions divided by qualified impressions, not clicks divided by all visits. Do not rank categories with zero impressions or incomparable coverage. Include measurement scope, activation time, denominators and unknown/unavailable states. Small samples are not a recommendation; require enough support to interpret differences. No numerical target is justified before real baseline.

Never store account IDs, raw IP addresses, search terms, free text, URLs, sensitive category reading histories, or join editorial events to geography, account, payment or existing audience tokens. Review replay protection, aggregation, deletion, retention, cost limits and tests before release. Start with a fixed, bounded aggregate transport behind the verified production consent and abuse controls; a separate opt-in does not imply authorization for unbounded tracking. Save/share signals require a confirmed successful action rather than an attempted click. Detailed card-level ranking and paid conversion attribution require separate privacy and measurement review; do not infer them from current aggregate counts.

Repeat browser share uses repeatBrowsers / visitors over the existing selected overlapping-session window. Zero denominator is not 0%. Under 20 measured browsers is labeled a small sample (a display heuristic, not a statistical guarantee). It is not D7 retention, distinct people, or evidence that content caused a return. Profiles remain separate. Revenue metrics must be read from verified Stripe aggregates, separated by currency, excluding test transactions and distinguishing gross, refunds, net and recurring commitments.

## Offline aggregate interpretation candidate

The proposed first increment is a pure, disconnected TypeScript interpreter with synthetic test fixtures. It accepts a complete closed UTC day from a single fixed public-home opt-in scope, exact taxonomy and version, and activation no later than day start. All five proposed pilot category cells must be explicit, including zero counts. The pilot subset is education, environment, science and technology, sport, and weather; this is a conservative lab proposal, not a privacy-reviewed production allowlist. Broad topics may still contain sensitive stories; a separate content and privacy review is mandatory before collection.

The descriptive rate is attributable opens / qualified impressions. Zero exposure, fewer than 100 qualified impressions, incomplete coverage and invalid data never become a valid numeric rate. The 100-impression display heuristic is not a statistical guarantee, contributor count, anonymity threshold or success target. Maximum one million impressions per category per day is a parser bound, not a storage budget. No cross-day pooling, individual attribution, segmentation, user-level data, rankings or automatic editorial action. Input shape validation cannot verify the producer's claims, consent, coverage, or the absence of replay; no live provider may feed this contract yet.

Subsequent separate gates: define the explicit user-facing open action in the actual reader and qualify visibility in real browser tests, design independent consent and withdrawal (including in-flight requests), decide a privacy-reviewed aggregation and suppression model resistant to reidentification and differencing, prove transport replay and rate controls, retention and deletion, cost ceilings and provider provenance, then verify private read-only admin integration. Neither this lab nor the previous general audience consent authorizes any new telemetry. Do not mount routes, write cookies, request production credentials, modify storage, change feed ranking or surface synthetic metrics in DLKT Control.

## Independent consent lifecycle — offline candidate 02

This increment introduces a disconnected, in-memory state machine for a purpose-specific choice. It deliberately cannot read or inherit general audience consent, persist a choice, mint a token, issue HTTP requests or activate collection. The notice version is a laboratory placeholder, not approved legal copy. Production activation is explicitly false in every state.

A fresh explicit editorial choice and a current privacy/readiness context start a pending grant. Only an exact matching synthetic coordinator receipt moves it to the granted laboratory state. GPC, DNT, missing or malformed context, obsolete notice, lost readiness and explicit withdrawal close the gate synchronously. An ambiguous grant response requests cleanup, rather than automatically retrying or assuming nothing happened. A late or duplicate grant acknowledgment cannot reopen the gate. Withdrawal retries reuse the current cleanup command, and re-enabling requires a fresh explicit choice after confirmed cleanup. Withdrawal confirmation remains possible while all collection gates are closed.

Local bigint revisions are strictly in-memory operation counters, not persistent identifiers, cross-tab locks, server tokens or a network protocol. Receipts in this module are synthetic and shape-checked, not cryptographic or authenticated evidence. A separate verified coordinator must implement durable purpose/version binding, real trusted-gesture and UI boundaries, cross-tab locking, expiry, server-side atomic revocation and write fencing, recovery after browser restart or lost response, retention/deletion semantics, abuse limits and no pre-consent activity. The lifetime, storage model, exact data disclosures and lawful basis require separate review. Do not mount the laboratory controller, treat its simulated state as proof of consent or reuse existing audience grants. No new collection, cookie, storage or public notice is added.

## Qualified Exposure Engine — offline candidate 03

The actual global-news title and Open source controls both link to the original publisher in a new tab. They are outbound source-opening attempts, not an on-site article open, a successful publisher page load, or evidence of reading. Do not populate attributableOpens with these actions or zero placeholders. The aggregate interpreter is not yet connected to a producer; a separate metric/version and an approved action definition are required before any open-rate report can become live.

The disconnected browser laboratory measures only the denominator: at least half the entire card rectangle intersects the viewport for at least 1,000 ms of continuously qualifying observation, with foreground focus and no open dialog. It conservatively restarts dwell after geometry or document changes, gaps over 250 ms, scroll, blur or backgrounding. Cards taller than twice the viewport can legitimately remain unqualified. Intersection and geometry measurements are heuristics, not proof of human attention, unobscured pixels or reading. Only global stream markup matching exact article identity and the proposed category subset is considered. Stable story keys (otherwise article keys) deduplicate rerenders within one ephemeral document; changes in grouping between editions and page reloads are not yet solved. Category changes after completion, duplicates, malformed input or capacity overflow hold the result. The 500-card cap is a laboratory memory bound; no persistent card history or identifier leaves the module. Withdrawal discards local counts; no network, browser storage, user identifiers, public asset, route, or feed change is introduced.

The pilot category definition is shared with the aggregate interpreter. Ten new node regressions and an additional Chromium loopback fixture are added to existing CI. The fixture uses synthetic markup matching the reader contract, not live publisher content. It exercises no consent, short dwell, qualified visibility, rerender deduplication, modal suspension, oversized-card exclusion and withdrawal. Until exact-head CI completes, none of these new checks is claimed passed. Still required: actual rendered reader integration and performance checks, reviewed metric/action definition, privacy/consent and retention approval, authenticated server coordination, bounded aggregate transport and suppression, replay and revocation protection, and private admin readback. No production activation is authorized.

## Secure Aggregate Transport — offline candidate 04

Exact corrected candidate 03 c6d12d36 passed all four workflows, 86 packaging plus 2,735 source tests, no failures or skips, and the Chromium loopback exposure fixture reported two qualified impressions, short dwell/modal/oversize exclusion, rerender deduplication and withdrawal cleanup. These results validate controlled markup, not actual reader telemetry.

The new disconnected laboratory accepts one canonical UTF-8 exposure-only document vector per synthetic grant. Maximum 2,048 body bytes; exact five-category order; 1–500 total qualified impressions; UTC day and document-only coverage; no opens, article keys, geo, account/audience identifiers, or extra fields. Empty documents send nothing. A separate opaque random grant is issued internally from the synthetic consent coordinator, bound to its revision, current UTC day and at most 15 minutes. Only its domain-separated digest is indexed. Exact replay returns duplicate; conflicting replay fails; expired and revoked grants cannot write. The in-memory reference keeps reversible grant-linked vectors until cleanup/expiry, plus bounded closed tombstones. Lifetime limits are 256 issued grants and 128 accepted contributions per lab instance, not a hosted rate limit or cost estimate. A rollback of the supplied clock holds the instance and drops pending data. Expiry is evaluated on access, not an installed purge job; process exit loses all state. No sealed daily aggregate, verified coverage, production activation or reporting of interest is possible.

The unmounted HTTP handler accepts only explicit loopback POST paths, same-origin marker and explicit bearer, no ambient cookies or forwarded-host/proto, bounded canonical JSON, with GPC/DNT stopping intake. Withdrawal remains possible with privacy signals active. Fourteen synthetic node cases include real loopback requests. The synchronous store is only a single-process reference; separate workers, durable transactions, authenticated server-side consent and atomic revocation/ingestion, true global request admission, privacy review of temporarily linked vectors, retention/purge and contributor suppression are unresolved. Do not mount this handler in production or describe the grant-linked vectors as anonymous. No hosted database, source, reader, CSS, payments, production credentials or ranking changes.
