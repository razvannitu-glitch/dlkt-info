# DLKT Control: editorial measurement design

Status: decision record, not an activated data source. Initial release improves discoverability of the existing voluntary measurement choice, and derives the descriptive repeat-browser share from existing opt-in snapshots. No new tracking, cookie, identity linkage, retention extension or billing change is authorized by this release.

## Editorial intelligence: release gates

Existing audience consent covers page entries, recent activity, coarse geography and traffic sources. It does NOT silently extend to reading history, impressions, card selection, saves, shares or category interactions. A separately explained voluntary choice is required before collecting new interaction data; old consent must not migrate automatically. Reading and account use must remain unrestricted. Keep existing opt-in off by default, respect GPC/DNT and provide withdrawal.

Start with public-reader category-level aggregates, not personal interest profiles. Reuse the canonical editorial taxonomy only after review for sensitive categories. Measure a qualified impression (at least half a card visible continuously for one second while document is visible) and explicit open action for that same impression. Deduplicate rerenders and retries, exclude prefetch/background activity, keep actual opening distinct from impression and from outbound source click. A category's open rate is openings attributable to qualified impressions divided by qualified impressions, not clicks divided by all visits. Do not rank categories with zero impressions or incomparable coverage. Include measurement scope, activation time, denominators and unknown/unavailable states. Small samples are not a recommendation; require enough support to interpret differences. No numerical target is justified before real baseline.

Never store account IDs, raw IP addresses, search terms, free text, URLs, sensitive category reading histories, or join editorial events to geography, account, payment or existing audience tokens. Review replay protection, aggregation, deletion, retention, cost limits and tests before release. Start with a fixed, bounded aggregate transport behind the verified production consent and abuse controls; a separate opt-in does not imply authorization for unbounded tracking. Save/share signals require a confirmed successful action rather than an attempted click. Detailed card-level ranking and paid conversion attribution require separate privacy and measurement review; do not infer them from current aggregate counts.

Repeat browser share uses repeatBrowsers / visitors over the existing selected overlapping-session window. Zero denominator is not 0%. Under 20 measured browsers is labeled a small sample (a display heuristic, not a statistical guarantee). It is not D7 retention, distinct people, or evidence that content caused a return. Profiles remain separate. Revenue metrics must be read from verified Stripe aggregates, separated by currency, excluding test transactions and distinguishing gross, refunds, net and recurring commitments.

## Acceptance

Existing Personalise dialog has a plain link to optional audience choices and the footer preserves the canonical Cookies link; no overlay, preselected option, pre-consent identifier or new request. Preserve current reader layout and styles. Existing repeat-browser count remains, with honest descriptive share and unavailable/zero/sample states. ## Production candidate — actual reader source-intent boundary (issue #104)

The isolated experiments in PR #103 are complete and deliberately not merged. This separate candidate starts from production main 2005ad5c. It implements a distinctly versioned, in-memory source-intent cohort for the actual canonical homepage reader. It is not a new data source and no code mounts it in the public package. The only mount is a loopback QA import of the compiled module on the real reader generated by NewsPageService over controlled corpus fixtures.

A future independently authenticated consent coordinator must supply the exact purpose/notice/eligibility/revision and maximum 15-minute document permit and synchronously stop the controller on withdrawal; the shape is not proof of consent. The fixture supplies a synthetic permission only. No account or general audience consent is read. Missing/changed/expired permit, GPC/DNT and clock regression stop the controller. No pre-consent identifier, browser storage, cookie, network transport, server route or public notice is added. No automatic remount on renewal. Real cross-tab/server consent fencing remains a release gate.

The provisional five-category scope uses exact global stream card metadata and the two same-destination HTTPS primary publisher anchors, excluding local/owned/saved content. Half of the entire card rectangle must be in the viewport for a continuous sampled second in the focused visible homepage with no open modal. Oversized cards can remain unqualified; the metric is geometry-based, not proof of unobscured pixels or reading. At most one trusted primary click (including keyboard-generated click or touch tap) per previously qualified logical card, excluding programmatic clicks, middle-click/context-menu, non-primary links, prequalification and repeated activations. It does not claim successful publisher navigation. The same article/story identity and category must remain consistent; changing grouping or primary identity holds the entire document. The 500 eligible-identity limit bounds ephemeral deduplication. No card ID or URL is emitted. Category aggregate fields are qualifiedImpressions and sourceIntents, never the older attributableOpens or unique readers.

Eight new reducer/contract tests and additional native Chromium scenarios on the actual built reader at 390 and 1440px cover prequalification, programmatic versus trusted gestures, same-destination deduplication, withdrawal, GPC and preservation of original nodes. Test-only document listener prevents external navigation after the observer sees the native intent. No external publisher request or actual user is required. Full exact-head CI must pass before any validation claim. Actual reader performance under larger feeds, mobile variants, real consent UI and coordinator, approved sensitive-content exclusion, reviewed source-intent wire schema/aggregation, retention/withdrawal and abuse/cost controls remain unresolved. No production authorization, public asset, CSS, database, payment, source or editorial ranking changes.

### Actual reader binding correction

Exact head 33e0c8d4 passed release verification (86 packaging + 2,715 source tests), live reader and freshness, but failed the new 390px offline Chromium exposure scenario. Source inspection established the mismatch: continuationItems supplies article:<card.identity> for single articles, which may contain the original publisher URL, whereas the candidate required all present story attributes to be story:<64 hex>. The correction normalizes bounded article presentation fallbacks to the independently validated short data-card-id/data-news-stream-key pair and preserves valid grouped story keys. No raw fallback identity is retained or emitted. Two additional regression tests cover the actual continuationItems projection and malformed/grouped cases; the browser fixture now asserts canonical identity binding and supplies bounded geometry/status diagnostics on failure. The original qualification threshold, dwell and native-gesture assertions remain unchanged. Exact-head browser success is required to establish that the correction resolves the observed timeout; source inspection alone is not a successful runtime test.
