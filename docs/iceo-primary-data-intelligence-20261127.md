The [linked licence](https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence) permits commercial and non-commercial use subject to conspicuous attribution, exclusions including personal data and third-party rights, no implied endorsement, indemnity and other terms. Not blanket permission for every HDB webpage or automated request. [HDB producer release](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/hdb-pulse/news/2026/3rd-quarter-2026-public-housing-data-and-upcoming-flat-supply), dated 23 October 2026, explicitly identifies Q3 2026 median resale tables, establishing a newer official period than the catalogue. The [HDB methodology](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/selling-a-flat/process-for-selling-a-flat/resale-flat-planning/resale-statistics) describes town/flat-type medians of registered resale cases, with certain atypical transactions excluded, dash for no transactions and asterisk for fewer than 20 cases (suppressed). No PDF contents used. The four-column catalogue has no separate currency field; SGD is the local currency convention for this narrowly scoped Singapore HDB pilot, to be independently confirmed in the technical dataset review.

No valid API response, full CSV, dataset completeness, robots decision, automated-collection permission or authenticated remote content hashes were obtained. The JSON evidence packet therefore records ten manually transcribed public catalogue preview rows only. Five numeric medians, two NA markers and three dashes; this is not representative of the entire dataset. Preserve every marker and the exact three-dimensional grain. ## Operator-only calculation and freshness gate

The bounded offline mechanism reuses the exact #110 source preflight and its historical audit model. It validates source/dataset/producer binding, exact fixed evidence dates, quarter boundaries, composite uniqueness, exact four-column schema, positive integer price strings, missing/suppression markers, and rejects new fields or geography outside the two-town pilot. Compare only the same quarter and flat type between two towns. Example from the public 2007-Q2 preview: 4-room Ang Mo Kio 260000 and Bedok 224500, difference 35500 in local dollars. This is a historical difference of two medians, never a current price, market valuation, recommendation or pooled median. Attribution binds the dataset, producer, access date and current linked licence. No raw individual transaction data used.
