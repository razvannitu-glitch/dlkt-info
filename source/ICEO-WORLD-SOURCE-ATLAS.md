Status: operator-reviewed discovery CANDIDATES only. No source becomes connected or licensed by appearing here. Source: DLKT INFO GitHub, 23 November 2026.

## The completeness contract

No static list can enumerate absolutely every current source: new local publishers, offline notices, official portals, and changing social channels arise continuously. Build a source-discovery mesh that grows from Media Cloud Directory (more than 25,000 tracked media sources), GDELT global media monitoring, OpenAlex Sources for scholarly venues (not news), official government directories, approved APIs, OpenStreetMap for location metadata, and direct verified local partners.

Maintain separate counters for discovered, rights-reviewed, approved, connected, checked in the last hour, accepted, disabled, and surfaced sources. Do not claim that registered sources are live or that GDELT/Media Cloud licences grant the right to copy each publisher's article, image or full text. Original links and independent summary text are the default.

## Geographic feed ICEO — zone / city / country / worldwide

Each ingested event must be associated with a separately verified affected area: district, neighborhood or zone only when the source actually supplies evidence; then city, country and international. Publisher headquarters is provenance, never proof of where an event occurred. Unknown affected areas stay unknown. Geography is not inferred merely from the reader IP or the language of the article.

Random but ordered means a deterministic seed based on edition, local calendar date, language and user-selected city. Rotate sources and topics inside each locality band while preserving global discovery and an infinite-scroll signed cursor. Append new records without reshuffling already read items. A single event should have one primary card with alternative original sources underneath. Canonical URLs and event identifiers remove hard duplicates; cross-language semantic matches require stringent event, place and time consistency. Absolute semantic zero-duplicates cannot be guaranteed before review.

## Coral Impact — partner and user contract

First integrated informational pilot uses independent DLKT copy linked to three official WHO initiatives: breast cancer, cervical cancer and voluntary blood donation. WHO name and site link identify factual sources, NOT an endorsement or partnership. No WHO logos or unlicensed graphics are copied, no donations or sensitive health profiles are collected. Source-link cards may ask the user to read reputable information or consult an authorised local health service.

Potential Romanian organizations for discussions, not contracted partners: Fundatia Renasterea for medical screening, Daruieste Viata for hospital infrastructure, and Asociatia Little People for paediatric cancer psychosocial support. Confirm identity, permission for brand assets, campaign date, recipient bank/processor, geographical eligibility, call-to-action truth, healthcare review and budget. A donor must give directly to the legitimate partner unless a separately licensed and audited funds flow is implemented.

Partner commercial options: clearly labeled cause sponsorship, creative and information production service, annual campaign placement or operational reporting. Independently verified sources must never be ranked for payment. Impact indicators: valid views, source/partner CTA open rate, genuinely confirmed participation, partner-reported aggregate outcomes, cost per verified action and number of complaints. Health-related retargeting, condition inference and sale of sensitive data are prohibited.

## Operational launch sequence

1. Import catalog entries from authorized global directories and official national lists into a staging area, never the live fetch allowlist.
2. Verify URL ownership, licensing, commercial use, text/photo permissions, public-data relevance, regional jurisdiction and source reliability.
3. Approve exact endpoints and rate limits, then monitor check freshness, duplicates, contradictions and origin failures; disable invalid sources automatically.
4. Roll out local-first and worldwide card variety with transparent coverage metrics by country, language and actual event geography.
5. Run accessibility, high-concurrency, load, legal and editorial checks before claiming universal reach or monetized partner integrations.
