# ICEO World Source Atlas & Coral Care

Status: source-backed engineering inventory, 23 November 2026. This is a source-discovery and governance contract, not a claim of universal coverage, a contract with every publisher, or evidence that each listed source currently returns eligible records. Canonical platform: https://www.dlkt.ee/. All branding and public card copy remain in the existing English/Romanian/Italian localized format.

## The actual open-world requirement

No finite list is equivalent to "every source on Earth". Publishers, public datasets, community organizations and hyperlocal notices change every day. An exhaustive active ingestion promise is neither technically demonstrable nor legally safe. ICEO must operate an open-ended candidate index, distinguish discovery from permission and actual ingestion, and measure remaining geographic/linguistic gaps rather than hide them.

Configured feeds are definitions in ICEO_SOURCES; their status depends on runtime availability and rights review expiry. Reviewed publisher origins are identity allowlists, not feed connectivity or reproduction licences. Candidate and discovered origins are research-only and never passed to outbound acquisition automatically. The catalog may have duplicate domains where several distinct editorial feeds are registered. Its current count is an inventory metric, not active feed count.

Use discovery indexes like GDELT, Common Crawl, ReliefWeb and specialized portals to propose sources without copying content or asserting licensing. Rights clearance and independent official source checks precede activation; no unauthorized crawling of protected sites, paywalls, closed portals, private addresses or login areas. Prefer original URLs, titles, timestamps and short authored summaries with source attribution. Follow the site's robots policy, rate limits, copyright, GDPR and source-specific terms. No unchecked external user URL is sent to the backend.

## Geographic reading without fraud

Keep one seamless infinity reader and only verified event geography. The conceptual order is immediate locality, neighbourhood/zone, city, country, and worldwide. At present, the service can identify supported selected-city reports, national publisher provenance and international coverage. It does not possess general postal-code/street-level event evidence for every country and cannot label a publisher's headquarters as the event's neighbourhood. Unverified geography stays explicitly unknown. Missing local content is replaced with source-qualified broader news, never invented local news.

All local/zone/city/national/international groups share one event identity policy. Canonical source IDs, links, normalized titles and compatible event intervals form conservative candidate groupings. Conflicting identities are quarantined, rather than forcing a merge. An event mentioned by several outlets should be shown once with additional original source links. Fresh developments on a continuing event can be a new revision without duplicating the unchanged previous card. A semantic duplicate rate of absolute zero cannot be guaranteed until measurable against a labeled corpus; report duplicate complaints, false merges and rejected upstream records.

Variety is seeded deterministically per edition. Rotate subject category, publisher family, event geography and content type, without randomizing dates or inventing facts. Cap repeated publisher exposure and philanthropy-card saturation. The existing user-approved knowledge rhythm remains one insertion after 22 eligible original news items; the new Coral Care entries participate in that finite reviewed collection. The first eligible Coral Care entry is promoted to the first existing knowledge slot; no extra card is invented and the original news order remains unchanged.

Local: city halls, district councils, rural communes, neighbourhood associations, public notices, public libraries, museums, local events, official transit operators, police and emergencies, public schools, clinics, utilities, municipal budget portals, opening hours from primary sites, verified community organizers and local nonprofits.

National: public administration, parliament, official journal, consumer protection, economic statistics, central bank, public-health institutes, weather agency, rail and road authority, election bodies, courts and public registers, university networks, trade organizations, official tourism, cultural institutions, national sport federations, press and specialized newsrooms.

Worldwide: approved original news origins; regional and international public bodies; health authorities; validated research databases; science and space agencies; weather and hazards; trade, economic and education datasets; culture and history archives; biodiversity and environmental records; humanitarian networks; verified independent NGO campaigns; local and global professional communities. User-submitted organizations must go through exactly the same review gate.

## Coral Care — independent help and cause awareness

Use #F66061 only for verified cause-awareness cards, not for an inferred "good charity" rating or sponsored promises. The first four entries link to WHO's 2026 breast-cancer facts, Fundatia Renasterea's DARIA programme, HOSPICE Casa Sperantei's involvement information, and WHO's 2026 blood-donor campaign. The organization is named and sourced. DLKT does not take donations, process appointments, claim a partnership, provide diagnosis or use personal health records. The copy and destination must be re-reviewed on schedule. Sensitive medical recommendations defer to licensed health professionals and current jurisdiction-specific health guidance.

Potential partnerships are independent proposals. Any organization seeking a published "verified partnership" label must submit legal identity, ownership of the destination domain, privacy/contact disclosures, evidence of activity, current campaign terms, beneficiary and funds destination, transparent sponsor status, and a review expiry. Payment goes directly to the nonprofit or an authorized payment provider under its own contract. Do not put vulnerable people's health histories, beneficiary photos or donors' identifying information in the feed without legitimate basis and consent. An embedded checkout, campaign fund balance or real-time donation counter is out of scope until verified and implemented.

## Other high-value card formats to consider

The following are concepts, not activated source connectors or claims of content availability.

1. **AROUND YOU**. Only verified neighbourhood notices and tangible local changes, with precise provenance.
2. **CITY IN 60 SECONDS**. One researched practical city fact with official original link.
3. **BEFORE YOU GO**. Transport disruption, weather and road closure applicable to the selected route.
4. **PUBLIC MONEY NEARBY**. Verified municipal spending and dates from primary budget records.
5. **PUBLIC DEADLINE**. Permit, school, tax and benefit deadlines with eligibility and official source.
6. **RIGHTS IN A CARD**. A consumer or civic right and exactly where the official procedure is published.
7. **PRICE REALITY**. Verified local market basket or price evolution with units and dates.
8. **RECALL RADAR**. Affected product identifier, batch, country and authoritative recall advisory.
9. **AIR & WATER WATCH**. Current environmental quality data with measuring station and timestamp.
10. **TODAY IN YOUR AREA**. Local public event with organizer, venue and calendar day.
11. **WEEKEND WITHIN REACH**. Travel and culture ideas with official timetables, no invented seats or prices.
12. **LOCAL HELP DESK**. Verified local nonprofit service, requirements and official point of contact.
13. **AROUND THE WORLD**. A significant sourced development from an underrepresented country.
14. **TUVALU TO THE WORLD**. Under-covered microstate developments without fictional urgency.
15. **WORLD IN ONE FACT**. A cross-national comparison using matched datasets and time periods.
16. **WHAT CHANGED TODAY**. Source-dated change since last checked, not an invented personal memory.
17. **BEFORE AND AFTER**. A policy or rule revision with original versions and effective dates.
18. **UNDER THE RADAR**. Useful lower-visibility verified reporting, with reasons for selection.
19. **ONE EVENT MANY SOURCES**. A deduplicated event with links to different independent origins.
20. **FACT VS ASSUMPTION**. A claim compared against primary evidence; uncertainty displayed.
21. **THIS DAY HERE**. Verified local historical event, original archives and calendar locality.
22. **THIS DAY IN THE WORLD**. Historical anniversary tagged as history, not breaking news.
23. **BORN TODAY**. Historical birthday linked to an authoritative biographical source.
24. **REMEMBERING TODAY**. Historical death anniversaries without sensationalism.
25. **DID YOU KNOW**. Curated science, society and geography facts with reviewed source.
26. **THE USEFUL CONNECTION**. A verified non-obvious relationship between two facts.
27. **TODAY'S RECIPE**. Original researched cooking guidance with source and allergens.
28. **WHAT IS IN SEASON**. Local seasonal ingredients without false availability or pricing.
29. **FOOD SAFETY FIRST**. Official safety alerts and storage guidance.
30. **LABEL DECODER**. What a regulated ingredient, logo or nutritional label actually means.
31. **FAMILY TODAY**. Official child education and family service information.
32. **LEARN ONE THING**. Short primary-sourced educational micro-lesson.
33. **SCHOLARSHIP WINDOW**. Eligibility, dates and original scholarship application.
34. **TECH WITHOUT HYPE**. A specific technical change confirmed at the primary publisher.
35. **CYBERSECURITY CHECK**. Security vulnerability and actionable original advisory.
36. **SCIENCE UPDATE**. A study with date, study type, limitations and DOI.
37. **SKY TONIGHT**. Astronomy event and viewing window from primary forecasts.
38. **SPORTS NEXT**. Verified next competition schedule, no invented live score.
39. **CULTURE NEARBY**. A verified exhibition or cultural performance.
40. **CORAL CARE / BREAST HEALTH**. Official screening information and access; no personalized medical advice.
41. **CORAL CARE / BLOOD**. Current official blood donation eligibility and regional service links.
42. **CORAL CARE / CHILDREN**. Verified pediatric support organizations and family services.
43. **CORAL CARE / PALLIATIVE**. Support services and validated volunteering information.
44. **CORAL CARE / RARE DISEASE**. Accredited patient-group resources and specialist support.
45. **CORAL CARE / ACCESSIBILITY**. Verified services and changes improving access for disabled people.
46. **CORAL CARE / MENTAL WELLBEING**. Official support contacts and evidence-based resources, never diagnosis.
47. **CORAL CARE / DISASTER RELIEF**. Validated aid operations and secure official ways to help.
48. **CORAL CARE / FOOD SOLIDARITY**. Accredited food banks and local volunteering.
49. **CORAL CARE / EDUCATION**. Verified literacy, tutoring or school-supply causes.
50. **THE OPPORTUNITY**. A real open procurement, role, grant or public call, with deadlines and terms.
51. **ZODIAC / ENTERTAINMENT ONLY**. Opt-in entertainment labeled as non-scientific and never mixed with factual alerts.
52. **A SMALL ACTION**. A measurable privacy-respecting action with a clear beneficiary.
53. **WORLD WINDOW**. A periodically rotating perspective from a geography missing from the previous edition.

## Candidate qualification engine — controlled increment, 23 November 2026

The pure, offline engine in `src/iceo/discovery-qualification.ts` accepts bounded third-party directory observations (maximum 200 per batch) from research indexes including GDELT, Media Cloud, OpenAlex and ReliefWeb. These names identify expected metadata provenance, NOT connected APIs, verified index records, permitted crawling, validated publisher ownership or live ingestion. No network request, public endpoint, schedule or new feed is added by this change.

Each proposal preserves an original HTTPS source origin, an index record reference with its query string removed, observation time, category inferred only from the type of index, and an explicitly unknown publication/event geography. Known origins, www aliases and duplicate proposals are not promoted. The batch rejects malformed origins, forged index hosts, stale/future observations and untrusted records. It neither authenticates the directory's identity nor verifies the underlying news article, and does not treat OpenAlex scholarly sources as a news feed.

A separate operator review checklist requires recorded publisher-identity evidence, terms/robots inspection, the declared rights basis, intended metadata/excerpt use and a non-expired review window. Passing the checklist means COMPLETE FOR HUMAN REVIEW only; the output always sets `activationAllowed: false`. The operator must independently verify evidence and follow the existing source-specific registry and rights-review controls before any acquisition. Candidate metadata must never be connected to the public card stream as if it were sourced news.

Acceptance tests validate no outbound network calls, no invented event country, no rights promotion, deduplication, source/index URL constraints, date restrictions and fail-closed review. The four existing Coral Care cards, visual interface, user navigation, paid systems and production configuration remain unmodified. A separate approved rights-reviewed provider adapter and controlled runtime connectivity test are required before a directory can power automatic discovery.
