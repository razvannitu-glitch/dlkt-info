# DLKT — canonical palette lock

The user-authorized DLKT palette contains exactly three direct product colors:

- Black — `#000000`
- White — `#FFFFFF`
- Coral — `#F66061`

No off-white, independent grey, secondary red, blue, green, purple or unrelated accent may enter the product design system. Neutral surfaces, borders, muted text, shadows and interaction states may use only alpha transparency or `color-mix()` combinations derived from these three canonical colors.

Coral is the identity signal. Coral backgrounds use black text where required for readable contrast. Small coral text on white may use an accessible dark mix derived exclusively from coral and black. Black surfaces use white text and coral signals.

This lock applies to the website, InfoCards, setup flows, favicon, PWA manifest, transactional templates, social cards and future clients. Any future color expansion requires an explicit brand decision.
