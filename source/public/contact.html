Contact DLKT

Write to office@dlkt.ee for a service enquiry. For privacy, use the subject “DLKT · Privacy request”; for a source concern, include “Correction or content rights”; for an accessibility barrier, include “Accessibility feedback”.

Include the page or card link and the information needed to understand the request. Please do not send passwords, access tokens or unrelated sensitive documents. Opening an email link creates no submitted message in DLKT; you decide whether to send it in your email app.
