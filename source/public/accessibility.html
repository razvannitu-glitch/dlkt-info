What the reader supports

The interface uses labelled controls, keyboard focus indicators, semantic headings and source dialogs. Date and location remain visible, with an explicit Use my location action and manual city selection. Weather and currency references are static controls. No moving headline ribbon is activated on the homepage. Additional news loads without changing the displayed prefix, and a labelled loading button provides a manual alternative.

Weather illustrations use gentle loops only for a current, supported forecast condition. They become static with reduced motion and pause when hidden, offscreen or covered by a dialog. The layout adapts to narrow screens. Saved choices can be used without a personal account, and failure of browser storage does not stop ordinary reading.

Known boundaries

We do not claim certified conformance to every accessibility standard. External publisher pages and downloadable source documents are controlled by their publishers and may have different accessibility. Source pages may require their own account, subscription or accessible alternative. Use the contact below if a DLKT control, contrast or layout blocks reading.

Report a barrier

Email office@dlkt.ee with the page, task you were trying, device/browser and any assistive technology relevant to the problem. Share only the information needed to describe it. The same published company contact is available on the contact page.
