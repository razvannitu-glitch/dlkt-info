Bucharest: heating, hot water and events

Termoenergetica Bucuresti (CMTEB): short factual summaries by sector from the public heating and hot-water status table at the official operator page. Restoration times are estimates, not confirmed outcomes. Street examples do not identify your address; household details are not reproduced. Each check expires after five minutes.

ARCUB: the official agenda and up to six linked event pages. Only explicitly published, matching years and times become dated schedules. Date-only ranges do not establish current opening hours. Confirm tickets and changes with the organiser. These sources do not cover every service or event in Bucharest.

The main daily top is a global selection, independent of the city section. A compact top highlights current publications from the last twenty-four hours. More for your day makes the remaining eligible reports available in successive pages, including specialist subjects and reports without a category label. Categories organize the coverage; they are not a whitelist or a limit on the total stream. Important global developments remain visible. Country context, a city you confirm and topics you explicitly select can influence the order. A headline place mention is labelled as a mention, not proof that an event affects you; the publisher’s home country is not treated as the event location. Selected city information appears separately below; worldwide publications do not fill missing local positions. Foreign bulletins retain their actual jurisdiction and do not imply that their rules apply to you.

Connected local journalism

Bucharest reporting currently uses B365 and Buletin de Bucuresti RSS feeds. Each displayed item must identify the city through an explicit place reference in its headline or bounded excerpt; publisher country alone is insufficient. Source checks expire after five minutes. Publication dates are preserved and do not establish when an incident occurred. Sources unavailable at the current check remain labelled unavailable.

B365: no more than 250 characters of headline and excerpt combined, linked to the original report. Buletin de Bucuresti: no more than 500 characters combined, with the named author, publisher, original link and CC BY 4.0 licence. Publisher photographs and full articles are not copied. Source terms were reviewed on 18 November 2026 and must be reviewed again by 18 December 2026.

The city selection considers freshness, practical subject matter, variety of publishers and variety of topics. Only identical normalized headlines or article URLs are grouped as duplicates. At most eight press reports, four per publisher and three per topic appear in the finite edition of at most twelve cards. These rules are not independent corroboration, a calibrated personal impact score or complete coverage of the most important news. Multiple CMTEB sector records may be grouped into one city overview without inferring your sector or building.

Two connected Bucharest publishers do not constitute a worldwide local-news network. Other cities retain the current connected coverage shown in the reader. Additional publishers require verified access, rights, geography and source behaviour before connection.

Local discovery and place references

Connected local information remains limited to the sources and cities shown by the reader. Optional OpenStreetMap places are city-centre reference points, credited to OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL, accessed through Private.coffee Overpass. They do not establish opening hours, current events, prices or availability. A small Bucharest fallback uses Wikidata CC0 structured-data references reviewed on 17 November 2026.

Direct links to an organiser, authority, publisher or map search are labelled as exploration. Those destinations are not represented as connected DLKT feeds, and their creative article text, titles and photographs are not republished through this feature.

How a card reaches the reader

The service reads reviewed, fixed publisher feeds and supported source documents. It checks source identity, format, required fields and freshness boundaries before displaying a record. Unsupported or unavailable material is not replaced with an invented story.

Source titles and bounded publisher excerpts remain attributed. Extracted passages are selected passages, not a complete report or a generated factual verdict. Comparisons describe supported changes between checks; absence from a feed does not establish that an event ended or a notice was withdrawn.

City and daily reference sources

MET Norway supplies city-centre forecasts under CC BY 4.0. GeoNames supplies the city catalog under CC BY 4.0. London Tube information uses TfL Open Data with the attribution shown in the reader.

Palma has selected municipal culture listings, council announcements and TIB transport notices. Other cities have different connected coverage, shown in the local view. This is not a nationwide or worldwide local-news network.

ECB reference exchange rates are available free from the ECB. DLKT identifies its calculations and keeps the reference date visible. Each source’s own terms and attribution remain relevant.

Personalisation and the daily ribbon

Your selected topics and city help determine the available briefing. The daily ribbon uses the same source-backed signals and pauses for reading. Read updates provides a static list and source details. No scrolling speed or visual emphasis is a confidence score.

The service does not represent all available publishers as independent corroboration of a claim. Sponsorship, if introduced, must be identified separately from editorial/source material. This release does not activate advertising or paid ranking.

Photographs and image credits

Selected publication cards include individually reviewed source photographs or source photo collages. When an individually reviewed source photograph is available it takes priority. Other cards use a separately reviewed contextual photograph, explicitly labelled Context photo and identified by subject; this does not establish a photograph of the reported event. The image source, creator and applicable license or media guidelines appear beside the image. A publisher text license does not automatically cover photographs by other creators.

Images are served by DLKT. Older photographs are identified in their captions, and source collages retain their composition. Context photographs are selected from their documented subjects and the card wording, not inferred personal traits. A missing image does not remove the publication text or source actions. The separate branded share download remains a text and source preview.

Consumer and cyber-security sources

The UK OPSS feed lists product safety alerts, reports and recalls. Its Updated date is the feed update, not necessarily the original recall date. Check the exact model, batch, affected country and instructions in the original. It does not cover every country or food, vehicles and medicines.

NCSC contributes UK cyber-security news under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Its stated audience and jurisdiction remain relevant; a news entry does not establish a personal breach or device vulnerability. No third-party images or logos are republished.

Shared cards can expose a source-dated DLKT preview image and a download link. Images include only public card metadata and the DLKT brand. A generic brand preview is used when the original title or required credits cannot be rendered faithfully. Open the card for complete source credits and the latest available check. Social services may cache their own previews.

A daily top with more to explore

The public edition monitors twenty editorial categories: World & geopolitics; Business & economy; AI & technology; Money & cost of living; Health & wellbeing; Sports & competitions; Gaming & esports; Culture & entertainment; Shopping & consumer products; Travel & flights; Work & careers; Education & learning; Family & parenting; Science & space; Climate & energy; Politics & rights; Digital life & security; Food & everyday living; Housing & cities; Weather & major disruptions. These are editorial choices informed by public-interest research, not a measured worldwide readership ranking.

The selection prioritises headlines describing a material development, then explicit country, confirmed-city and topic relevance. Category and publisher diversity guide a finite selection with a standard target of thirty and a maximum of forty. Essential developments can exceed the ordinary preferences of two cards per category and four per publisher; explicit interests do not remove them. Guides, opinion and listicles do not displace stronger developments just because they are newer or appear on a homepage. These are inspectable editorial rules, not a measured impact score or a guarantee of the most important news. Missing categories and a shortfall remain visible. An unavailable source is not an empty news day.

The top is followed by local information and a continuation of all remaining fresh, eligible publications in the current catalogue. Show more adds twenty at a time, without a fixed total ceiling. A publication can qualify with no category label and appear as Other topics; source, date and integrity checks still apply. Category labels are optional organisation, not an admission whitelist. Available records remain bounded by connected-source coverage and collection capacity.

Only original publication dates within the rolling twenty-four-hour window qualify for the public daily top. A source check, index appearance, modification or legal document date cannot make an older story new. Every publication retains its original date and publisher link. The separate reader can still expose other dated official records.

Discovery combines the GDELT Article List and reviewed publisher news indexes. Eligible URLs, headlines and dates are checked against the original publisher page. Bounded retained candidates help carry information between refreshes; each article still requires a new source check before admission. This is partial coverage of connected sources, not a search of every page on the web.

An article found among the first ten matching links inside the main content of a reviewed publisher homepage receives a placement signal. AP, The Guardian, PC Gamer and BBC are checked with strict time and size limits. This records the publisher placement observed at the check; it does not measure worldwide popularity, independently corroborate the story or certify its importance. Source availability, subject diversity, headline signals and recency determine the remaining order.

News discovery is scheduled every two minutes, with scheduling jitter and bounded collection time. Official feeds retain their own reviewed check intervals, which can be longer. Actual successful checks are recorded separately from the schedule. Public page requests read the shared catalogue and do not start another publisher collection. While visible, the page checks for updates every thirty seconds; failures back off and hidden pages stop polling. New or changed publications are announced through Show updates, leaving the current reading order stable. Expired publications leave the daily top. A source outage can temporarily preserve a recent successful check with its original timestamp; it never becomes a new verification. This is periodic discovery of connected sources, not an instantaneous worldwide news service. The candidate cache is bounded and separate from permanent shared-card history. The refresh sends no personal email or notification.

The news expansion displays attributed headlines, original publication dates and links. It does not republish article bodies or photographs, bypass paid access or turn an inaccessible article into a verified card. Original publishers retain their rights.

Community reporting and digital rights

Global Voices world and arts-and-culture feeds provide English-language community reporting and perspectives under CC BY 3.0. Original and translation credits from each feed entry are retained. Electronic Frontier Foundation Deeplinks provides digital-rights advocacy under CC BY 4.0 for its original material. These reviewed feeds can supply bounded excerpts under their stated terms; the broader news discovery shows headline metadata and original links.

Author and licence credits remain visible in the reader, shared card and downloaded reading copy. Duplicate record IDs, canonical article URLs and identical normalized headlines do not create extra daily positions. Conflicting identities are isolated. Publisher statements, advocacy, reporting and research retain their provenance; placement is not evidence of independent agreement.

Corrections, copyright and concerns

Email office@dlkt.ee with the DLKT page/card link, original-source link, relevant date and a concise description of the issue. For a rights claim, explain the material and your relationship to it; do not include unnecessary sensitive documents.

We can review DLKT’s extraction, attribution and presentation. A correction to an original publication may also require contacting its publisher. A complaint is assessed on its evidence; submitting one does not automatically establish that a claim is false or that you own the material.
