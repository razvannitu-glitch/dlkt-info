Bucharest: heating, hot water and events

Termoenergetica Bucuresti (CMTEB): short factual summaries by sector from the public heating and hot-water status table at the official operator page. Restoration times are estimates, not confirmed outcomes. Street examples do not identify your address; household details are not reproduced. Each check expires after five minutes.

ARCUB: the official agenda and up to six linked event pages. Only explicitly published, matching years and times become dated schedules. Date-only ranges do not establish current opening hours. Confirm tickets and changes with the organiser. These sources do not cover every service or event in Bucharest.

The news stream includes eligible global and local articles in the chosen reading language, arranged in shuffled category rounds. Each available category appears once per round; available national-edition reporting comes before international reporting within each category. City weather, service notices and other practical records remain in a separate city section below the news. A headline place mention is not proof that an event affects you, and the publisher's home country is not treated as the event location. Foreign bulletins retain their actual jurisdiction.

Connected local journalism

Bucharest reporting currently uses B365 and Buletin de Bucuresti RSS feeds. Each displayed item must identify the city through an explicit place reference in its headline or bounded excerpt; publisher country alone is insufficient. Source checks expire after five minutes. Publication dates are preserved and do not establish when an incident occurred. Sources unavailable at the current check remain labelled unavailable.

B365: no more than 250 characters of headline and excerpt combined, linked to the original report. Buletin de Bucuresti: no more than 500 characters combined, with the named author, publisher, original link and CC BY 4.0 licence for the licensed text. Full article bodies are not copied. Original-article photo previews follow the method below; a text licence is not applied to a photograph. The local text-source terms were reviewed on 18 November 2026 and are due for review again by 18 December 2026.

Eligible local reports in the chosen reading language join the continuous news stream through the same category rounds, without an eight-report limit or a per-publisher or per-topic display quota. Exact record IDs and article URLs are deduplicated; distinct original articles remain available even when their headlines match. Freshness, identity and source checks still apply, and connected-source coverage remains partial. Multiple CMTEB sector records may be grouped into one practical city overview without inferring your sector or building.

Two connected Bucharest publishers do not constitute a worldwide local-news network. Other cities retain the current connected coverage shown in the reader. Additional publishers require verified access, rights, geography and source behaviour before connection.

Local discovery and place references

Connected local information remains limited to the sources and cities shown by the reader. Optional OpenStreetMap places are city-centre reference points, credited to OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL, accessed through Private.coffee Overpass. They do not establish opening hours, current events, prices or availability. A small Bucharest fallback uses Wikidata CC0 structured-data references reviewed on 17 November 2026.

Direct links to an organiser, authority, publisher or map search are labelled as exploration. Those destinations are not represented as connected DLKT feeds, and their creative article text, titles and photographs are not republished through this feature.

How a card reaches the reader

The service reads reviewed, fixed publisher feeds and supported source documents. It checks source identity, format, required fields and freshness boundaries before displaying a record. Unsupported or unavailable material is not replaced with an invented story.

Source titles and bounded publisher excerpts remain attributed. Extracted passages are selected passages, not a complete report or a generated factual verdict. Comparisons describe supported changes between checks; absence from a feed does not establish that an event ended or a notice was withdrawn.

City and daily reference sources

MET Norway supplies city-centre forecasts under CC BY 4.0. GeoNames supplies the city catalog under CC BY 4.0. London Tube information uses TfL Open Data with the attribution shown in the reader.

Palma has selected municipal culture listings, council announcements and TIB transport notices. Other cities have different connected coverage, shown in the local view. This is not a nationwide or worldwide local-news network.

ECB reference exchange rates are available free from the ECB. DLKT identifies its calculations and keeps the reference date visible. Each source’s own terms and attribution remain relevant.

Country editions and daily reference signals

The selected city determines the country edition and the separately available local information. Within each news category, reporting from reviewed publisher editions in that country comes before eligible international reporting. This describes publisher provenance, not the event location. Source dates and freshness checks still apply. Optional personal briefings can use selected topics. Static weather and ECB reference controls keep practical signals separate from the article stream; the homepage does not label ordinary headlines as breaking news.

The service does not represent all available publishers as independent corroboration of a claim. Sponsorship, if introduced, must be identified separately from editorial/source material. This release does not activate advertising or paid ranking.

Photographs and image credits

A news card can show an individually reviewed source photograph bound to that exact article, or an image identified in that article's original publisher metadata. DLKT does not substitute a category image, stock photograph, contextual picture or unrelated portrait. The publisher may itself use an archive photograph, collage or illustration; an original-article preview does not establish that the image depicts the reported event.

Article-photo previews are served by DLKT in a uniform 16:9 frame with proportional cropping, without stretching. The Source photo link leads to the original article, where publisher captions, the full image and rights information can be checked. Separately reviewed photographs retain their recorded credits. DLKT does not invent an author or infer a photo licence from the article text, metadata or source link. If the original image is unavailable or fails to load, the article title and source actions remain visible. The separate branded share download remains a text and source preview.

Consumer and cyber-security sources

The UK OPSS feed lists product safety alerts, reports and recalls. Its Updated date is the feed update, not necessarily the original recall date. Check the exact model, batch, affected country and instructions in the original. It does not cover every country or food, vehicles and medicines.

NCSC contributes UK cyber-security news under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Its stated audience and jurisdiction remain relevant; a news entry does not establish a personal breach or device vulnerability. Article-photo previews follow the original-image method above and do not acquire a licence from the text attribution.

Shared cards can expose a source-dated DLKT preview image and a download link. Images include only public card metadata and the DLKT brand. A generic brand preview is used when the original title or required credits cannot be rendered faithfully. Open the card for complete source credits and the latest available check. Social services may cache their own previews.

News categories and continuous reading

The public news stream uses the 17 top-level IPTC Media Topics: arts, culture, entertainment and media; crime, law and justice; disaster, accident and emergency incident; economy, business and finance; education; environment; health; human interest; labor; lifestyle and leisure; politics and government; religion; science and technology; society; sport; conflict, war and peace; weather. The concept identifiers and standard names come from IPTC Media Topics. DLKT infers an article's category from canonical publisher sections and keywords, headline wording and supported source hints. This is an editorial inference, not an IPTC certification of each article. Short reader labels and Romanian translations are DLKT's.

The news stream cycles through all currently available categories in a shuffled order. Each active category contributes one article before the next round starts. When another category is available, the first article of a new round differs in category from the preceding article. Categories with no remaining articles leave later rounds; when only one remains, all its remaining articles stay available. The category order stays stable for the displayed edition while more cards load. A category filter shows a projection of that same order. The selected country gives available national-edition articles priority within each category. Other editorial signals order articles inside each national or international group; saved interests do not silently remove articles from the public stream. The reading language still applies: DLKT selects available reporting in its original language, and English remains an explicit option. Choosing English does not translate other-language articles. Headline developments, source observations, freshness and diversity inform editorial priority; it is not a measured worldwide popularity or importance ranking.

The stream retains every eligible, unique article from the available global and local catalogue. Category rounds affect order, not membership. Up to twenty articles are displayed initially, and reaching the end loads the next group of up to twenty automatically where supported. A labelled button remains available for manual loading. Category filters show matching and overall counts without imposing a total display ceiling. Articles without a supported category remain available as Other topics. Source, date and integrity checks remain mandatory; the catalogue is bounded by connected-source coverage and collection capacity.

The global publication selection uses original publication dates within a rolling twenty-four-hour window. A source check, index appearance, modification or legal document date cannot make an older story new. Local reports retain their separately reviewed publication and checking windows. Every article keeps its original date and publisher link, and other dated official records remain available through the relevant reader views.

Discovery combines the GDELT Article List and reviewed publisher news indexes. A newly admitted article requires its URL, headline and original publication date to be checked against the original publisher page. Bounded candidate rotation progresses across refreshes. Earlier verified articles may remain while still eligible, with their original observation timestamps and content hashes; retention is not a new source check. An original selected for reverification is not restored from retained data if that verification fails. This is partial coverage of connected sources, not a search of every page on the web.

An article found among the first ten matching links inside the main content of a reviewed publisher homepage receives a placement signal. AP, The Guardian, PC Gamer and BBC are checked with strict time and size limits. This records the publisher placement observed at the check; it does not measure worldwide popularity, independently corroborate the story or certify its importance. Source availability, subject diversity, headline signals and recency inform editorial priority within each category.

News discovery is scheduled every two minutes, with scheduling jitter and bounded collection time. Official feeds retain their own reviewed check intervals, which can be longer. Actual successful checks are recorded separately from the schedule. Public page requests read the shared catalogue and do not start another publisher collection. While visible, the page checks for updates every thirty seconds; failures back off and hidden pages stop polling. New or changed publications are announced through Show updates, leaving the current reading order stable. Expired publications leave the daily top. A source outage can temporarily preserve a recent successful check with its original timestamp; it never becomes a new verification. This is periodic discovery of connected sources, not an instantaneous worldwide news service. The candidate cache is bounded and separate from permanent shared-card history. The refresh sends no personal email or notification.

The news stream displays attributed headlines, original publication dates, source links and, when available, original-article photo previews as described above. It does not republish full article bodies, bypass paid access or turn an inaccessible article into a verified card. Original publishers and image owners retain their rights; a source link or displayed preview does not grant a reuse licence.

National and local coverage

The selected city supplies a reviewed country code. A national count means eligible articles from reviewed publisher editions for that country in the selected reading language. It is not a count of independently verified events inside that country. The local count uses separately verified city sources, and the international count describes the remaining eligible reports. These three counts describe the full current edition, not just its first page.

A country with no eligible national articles displays that shortfall and keeps available international reporting accessible. English is an explicit reading option, not an automatic translation service. The Italian national supply starts with the official la Repubblica RSS index, with each admitted article checked against its original publication metadata. One connected national publisher does not constitute complete coverage of Italian journalism or verified local reporting for Torino. Connected country coverage must expand through individually reviewed source access, identity, rights and freshness.

Community reporting and digital rights

Global Voices world and arts-and-culture feeds provide English-language community reporting and perspectives under CC BY 3.0. Original and translation credits from each feed entry are retained. Electronic Frontier Foundation Deeplinks provides digital-rights advocacy under CC BY 4.0 for its original material. These reviewed feeds can supply bounded excerpts under their stated terms; the broader news discovery shows headline metadata and original links.

Available author and licence credits remain visible in the reader, shared card and downloaded reading copy. Duplicate record IDs, article identities and canonical article URLs do not create extra stream positions. Distinct original reports remain available even when their headlines match. Conflicting identities are isolated. Publisher statements, advocacy, reporting and research retain their provenance; placement is not evidence of independent agreement.

Corrections, copyright and concerns

Email office@dlkt.ee with the DLKT page/card link, original-source link, relevant date and a concise description of the issue. For a rights claim, explain the material and your relationship to it; do not include unnecessary sensitive documents.

We can review DLKT’s extraction, attribution and presentation. A correction to an original publication may also require contacting its publisher. A complaint is assessed on its evidence; submitting one does not automatically establish that a claim is false or that you own the material.
