What DLKT does

DLKT brings source-linked news and practical information into one continuous reader. The opening page contains up to twenty eligible articles. Further pages follow while you read, with a manual loading control as well. The stream rotates through the standard IPTC news categories without repeating a category before the other available categories have appeared in that round. Every eligible article in the connected catalogue remains reachable; older articles are not added to fill space. Each card retains its original publisher, publication date and source link.

The selected city determines the country edition. Available reporting from reviewed publisher editions in that country comes first within each category, followed by international reporting in the selected reading language. National edition describes publisher provenance, not the location of every reported event. Local news requires separately verified city coverage. The reader shows national, local and international coverage and makes a missing national supply explicit. Date, location and optional city-centre weather and dated currency references are separate from the news cards. Use my location requests browser permission only after you choose it; manual city selection remains available.

Your selection, your browser

Reading is available without signing in. Save, Follow and Remember keep selected information in this browser. When account sign-in is available, your private profile can store optional name, language, timezone and interests, and you can explicitly import a reader copy into your account and restore it in another signed-in browser. This is not automatic cross-device sync. Followed categories remain in this browser. Paid checkout, a paid trial, scheduled briefing emails and background alerts are not activated.

Sources and geographic coverage are limited. DLKT does not search or verify every page on the web. See the method and connected sources.

Info Wallet

Info Wallet shows the current availability of account sign-in, private profile saving and optional email or social connections alongside the public daily briefing. When account sign-in is available, you can save optional Wallet preferences to your private account. Opening Wallet, signing in or saving a profile does not give DLKT access to your inbox or social accounts. An available connection requires your separate authorization with that provider.

When a provider connection is available and authorized, Check updates requests a bounded set of email metadata or comments. The homepage can show the five latest available updates and recheck while the page is visible and focused. This is not a complete activity history or background delivery. Account types, permissions and provider approval limits are explained in Wallet. Counts of views or shares do not identify everyone who viewed or shared a post. Open Info Wallet.

For organizations

Local, Teams and Public are pilot proposals. The organization setup can open a filtered reader preview, explicitly remember the setup in this browser and prepare an email enquiry for your review. Organization publishing, paid plans, team accounts and automated delivery are not activated. Explore the proposed plans.

Meet Kate

Kate is DLKT’s visual muse, representing curiosity, clarity and useful discoveries. Her symbol accompanies the reader; publisher names, source dates and coverage limits remain visible.

Who operates the service

DLKT is operated by DLKT Group OÜ, an Estonian company. Company details and contact routes are available without an account.
