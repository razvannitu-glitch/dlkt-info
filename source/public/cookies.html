Cookies & browser storage.

A practical inventory of the current reader and how to remove local choices.

The current cookie position

The current public reader does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not include third-party advertising or analytics scripts. Hosting/security mechanisms process request metadata and may use technical protection cookies when necessary. The privacy notice explains those purposes.

No optional advertising consent is requested because no advertising tracker is activated in this release. When account sign-in is available and you sign in, the first-party session cookies described below support your authenticated requests. Ordinary public reading does not require signing in.

Account session cookies

__Host-dlkt_info_at carries the access credential for authenticated account requests. Its browser lifetime is up to 1 hour: it is set to the provider-reported expiry minus 30 seconds, bounded between 60 and 3600 seconds.

__Host-dlkt_info_rt carries the refresh credential used to renew an authenticated session. Its browser lifetime is 30 days from when it is set or renewed. A cookie lifetime is not a guarantee that the server will accept the session for that entire period.

Both cookies use Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, with Path=/ and no Domain attribute. They are scoped to the host that sets them, sent over HTTPS and unavailable to page JavaScript. DLKT does not put these credentials in localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB. Signing out clears both cookies on that host and requests termination of that session; it does not sign out every other device or delete saved account data.

Connection authorization cookies

Choosing Connect for an available provider sets a temporary first-party cookie for that authorization: __Host-dlkt_connection_gmail, __Host-dlkt_connection_outlook, __Host-dlkt_connection_instagram or __Host-dlkt_connection_facebook_pages. It holds an unpredictable authorization-state value used to bind the callback to the connection request and signed-in session; it does not contain the provider access token.

Each authorization cookie has Max-Age=600, a lifetime of 10 minutes, and uses Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax with Path=/ and no Domain attribute. It is unavailable to page JavaScript and is cleared when the corresponding recognized callback is handled. The authorization request also expires after 10 minutes. Deleting this cookie can prevent an in-progress connection from completing; it does not delete an existing server-side connection.

What the browser can remember

IndexedDB reader memory stores explicitly remembered interests, followed editorial categories, saved source cards, comparison snapshots, a personal briefing and its feedback. A separate local-city store remembers a city and source comparison when you choose Remember city.

Daily-signal settings use local storage under dlkt-glance-preferences-1. They contain visibility choices and a reference currency. The ticker does not read or write a motion preference. Any dlkt-ticker-motion-v1 value left by an earlier version is ignored. These settings are separate from a browser’s location permission.

Followed topics

Follow on a card stores its displayed editorial category and, when available, a current source-listing comparison in the reader IndexedDB meta store under dlkt-category-follow-v1. These category choices and baselines stay in this browser; they are not included in account import, account restore or reading exports.

The homepage compares the existing public source snapshot against these saved checks while open. A reload does not advance the baseline. Save current check explicitly replaces the baseline for that category; Stop following removes that category and its baseline. Clear reader memory in Personalise removes all followed categories. This does not activate notifications, article translation or monitoring while the page is closed.

Reader appearance

The light/dark control remembers only your chosen appearance, Prism soft or Night coral, under dlkt-reader-theme-v1 in local storage. It writes only when you use that control; a first visit uses Prism soft without saving a choice. The value contains no city, interests, saved cards or account data and is not sent to DLKT.

Use the same control to change the appearance, or clear this origin’s site data in browser settings to remove the choice. It remains until changed or cleared; clearing reader or city memory is separate. If browser storage is unavailable, the control still changes the appearance for the current page.

Remove or change your choices

Open Personalise on the reader to review or clear reader memory, including remembered focus, followed categories, cards, comparison and personal briefing. Daily-signal preferences reset with that reader-clear action. Use Forget saved city in the local view to remove the separately remembered city and comparison.

You can also clear this site’s storage in your browser settings. www.dlkt.ee and info.dlkt.ee are separate browser origins, so clear each one you have used. Clearing local storage or session cookies does not remove your sign-in account, saved Wallet preferences, account reader copy or server-side connections. Use Disconnect in Wallet for an external account and remove provider authorization separately where instructed. Use their separate account controls or the privacy contact for those records. A downloaded reading copy or a file you shared is outside the browser store and must be managed separately.

Use Customize to change the daily signals without clearing saved cards. Revoke location permission in browser settings if you previously enabled it. Ordinary city search remains available without it.

Organization setup

The optional organization setup uses local storage under dlkt-organization-setup-v1 only after Remember this setup. It contains the selected pilot plan, source topics, reading length and optional text filter. It contains no organization name or email address. Use Clear saved organization setup on For organizations, or clear this origin’s site data, to remove it. Reader and city clearing are separate.

A preview link includes the selected source topics and filter in its URL fragment. Use public filter terms. No account, membership or organization verification is created by that link.

Duration and changes

Browser-local reading choices have no promised automatic deletion date; they remain until replaced, cleared or removed by the browser/device. Expired source content may stop appearing as current while a saved comparison remains on the device.

If optional tracking or a new storage purpose is introduced, its purpose, duration and applicable choice must be explained before activation. Read the privacy notice for hosting and enquiry data.
