      What these categories cover

      Twenty editorial categories span world affairs, money, technology, health, science, daily life, culture and sport. Each card identifies its publisher and original publication or update date.

      Only connected sources are included, so category coverage varies. The public top uses a rolling 24-hour window; personal topic results can also contain older records with their dates retained. A document date is not a publication date, and an opportunity notice does not prove an open application.
