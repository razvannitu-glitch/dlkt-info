Organization previews and pilot enquiries

Organization reading filters are processed by the reader on your device against the connected source response. Saving an organization setup is optional and browser-local, with its own clear control. An optional organization name is kept in the current form only and is included in a pilot draft only when you choose Prepare pilot request.

The draft is not submitted to DLKT by the form. Opening the email draft transfers it to your email app or provider; you choose whether to send it. A sent enquiry is processed like other correspondence described below. There is no activated checkout, organization publication service or paid membership.

Who is responsible

DLKT Group OÜ, Estonian registry 17335744, is the controller for processing it determines for DLKT. Registered office: Harju maakond, Tallinn, Lasnamäe linnaosa, Sepapaja tn 6, 15551, Estonia. For a privacy request, email office@dlkt.ee.

When you open or use the reader

Hosting and network systems process request information such as IP address, requested URL, time, browser or device information and security signals to deliver pages and APIs, troubleshoot failures and protect against abuse. A visit is not represented as anonymous merely because you do not have an account.

City search sends the text you enter to DLKT. On entry, an approximate current city supplied by the hosting network selects the initial public city edition. The city can be corrected; this estimate does not establish your address or neighbourhood. An explicit location action uses your browser’s permission and sends a rounded position to DLKT to suggest nearby city centres. Weather requests use the selected city’s centre, not a precise personal address.

Normal interest filtering and Save or Remember actions keep reading choices in the browser. An explicit account import, when available, stores a separate private reader copy as described below. Preparing a downloadable edition explicitly sends selected topics, a text filter and card count to DLKT for that response. Avoid putting confidential or sensitive personal details into search filters.

Account sign-in and private reader copies

When account sign-in is available, Supabase Auth processes the email address you submit, your account identifier, verification and sign-in timestamps, and session information needed to authenticate you. Requesting a code also sends your browser timezone (with UTC as a fallback) and the initial language setting, English. Creating an account creates a linked base profile using these values and account defaults. That base profile is separate from the optional Wallet preferences you choose to save. Requesting a sign-in code authorizes neither inbox access nor a subscription to briefing emails.

While signed in, Import from this browser explicitly sends a copy of your saved focus, source cards and comparison checkpoint to your private account. Restore in this browser explicitly replaces the corresponding local reader data with that account copy. These actions are not automatic background sync or a merge. Delete account reader copy removes that stored copy without deleting your sign-in account, Wallet preferences or existing browser copy.

Info Wallet and personal connections

The Wallet screen shows whether account sign-in and private profile saving are available. When account sign-in is available, a signed-in user can choose Save profile to store optional Wallet preferences privately in their account. The form contains an optional display name, language, timezone, preferred briefing time, chosen interests and a personal-first or balanced preference. Unsaved Wallet form edits are cleared when you leave the active view.

The preferred time is a preference, not a scheduled email or notification. Wallet preferences are kept separate from the base account profile, the account reader copy, public source cards and browser-local reader memory. Delete Wallet preferences removes those preferences; it does not delete the sign-in account, the account reader copy or browser reader memory.

Connection availability is shown in Wallet. Signing in or saving a profile does not grant access to an external account. Connecting an available provider requires a separate OAuth authorization in that provider’s account flow. Wallet explains supported account types, permissions, approval limits and available controls before you connect. Wallet profile details and connected activity are not included in public card links, downloadable public editions or shared source caches.

Optional email and social account access

When a connection is available and you authorize it, DLKT stores its provider, account label, connection status, connection time and last-check time in your private account. Provider access tokens, refresh tokens where supplied, and managed Facebook Page tokens are encrypted server-side before storage in Supabase. These provider credentials are not returned to page JavaScript or saved in browser storage.

Check updates performs a bounded read from the authorized provider. For an available and already-authorized connection, the signed-in homepage can also recheck about every sixty seconds while visible and focused, slowing down after failures. Gmail and Outlook supply email metadata such as sender, subject, time and a source link; this feature does not request message bodies or attachments. Instagram Business or Creator accounts and managed Facebook Pages can supply a limited sample of comments on their posts. The current feature does not retrieve personal direct messages, identify everyone who viewed or shared content, send emails, publish posts or modify comments. Instagram’s comment permission also permits moderation at the provider; DLKT uses it only to read comments.

Returned activity is processed by the DLKT server for the requested response and displayed transiently in the active Wallet or homepage account view. The homepage shows at most five recent metadata updates, with read status only when supplied by the provider. This connection feature does not save the returned email metadata or comment content to the account database or browser storage. The view is cleared when it loses focus, is hidden or left, when you sign out, or when its session can no longer be confirmed. A last-check timestamp is stored separately; it does not establish a complete activity history. This feature does not collect personal activity while the page is closed or provide scheduled personal delivery.

Disconnect removes the saved connection record and its encrypted credentials from DLKT. DLKT also requests provider revocation where supported; where this cannot be confirmed, Wallet instructs you to remove DLKT access in the provider’s account settings. Disconnect does not delete the original emails or comments, other saved DLKT data, or provider logs and backups. Signing out alone does not disconnect an external account.

What stays on your device

Saved focus, followed categories, cards, comparison checks, your personal briefing, explicit Useful/Not today feedback, a remembered city and daily-signal preferences are stored locally when you use the corresponding Save, Follow or Remember action. Local state can reveal your interests to another person using the same browser. An explicitly saved source check covers your chosen interests. Changes can be narrowed to saved card identities within those interests; this does not activate background monitoring or compare full article text.

DLKT’s public source cache is shared information, not an upload of your saved personal briefing. An account import is a separate explicit action. Local preferences are not sent as parameters to the public glance cache. The browser storage page describes the separate controls and retention.

Purposes and legal bases

The first local view may use an approximate city supplied by the hosting network. It is labelled approximate, can be changed, and is not saved as a preference automatically. The current connection estimate takes priority over an older saved city on a new visit. A city you choose during the visit takes priority over a later estimate; saved comparisons and personal reading stay separate. GPS is requested only after choosing the location control.

Delivery, troubleshooting and proportionate security processing serve our legitimate interests in operating a usable and secure information service under GDPR Article 6(1)(f). Optional location processing relies on your requested action and permission under Article 6(1)(a); you can withdraw the browser permission without losing ordinary city search. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Handling a legal obligation, including an applicable rights request, relies on Article 6(1)(c). Ordinary correspondence is processed under Article 6(1)(f), our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and resolving service concerns; correspondence needed for steps you request towards a contract may rely on Article 6(1)(b). Reading does not mean consenting to advertising or an unrelated data use.

Providers, links and international processing

Vercel supplies hosting, content delivery and operational/security infrastructure. Its service arrangements include data-processing terms and international transfer safeguards, including applicable contractual clauses. A European function region does not imply that every network, security or support operation stays in the EEA. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your request.

When account sign-in is available, Supabase provides account authentication and stores the base account profile, optional Wallet preferences and any reader copy you explicitly import. For a connection you authorize, Supabase also stores the connection record and encrypted provider credentials described above. Google, Microsoft or Meta processes the authorization and API requests for its corresponding connected service. Resend delivers transactional authentication emails, processing the recipient address, message content and delivery information. This does not connect your inbox to DLKT or activate marketing or scheduled briefing emails. Provider processing is distinct from the browser-local reading tools; a selected project region does not establish that every provider operation takes place in that region.

Source requests made by DLKT use fixed publisher endpoints and selected city coordinates where relevant. Mapped places, where available, use a bounded Private.coffee Overpass request with public city-centre coordinates, not your precise position. Data is credited to OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL. Bucharest may show a small curated Wikidata CC0 reference set, reviewed 17 November 2026, instead of a live map request result. Direct discovery searches send their query to the map or publisher only when opened. Opening an original-source link makes a separate connection to that publisher, whose own privacy terms apply. Choosing a sharing or email app also uses that app’s handling rules.

If you email us, your address, message and any attachments are processed by the company’s email service and the people handling the enquiry. We do not use the current reader to sell your saved reading profile or run advertising trackers.

How long information remains

Browser choices remain until replaced, removed with the corresponding control or cleared by the browser/device. Their source cards can expire for display without deleting every stored local record. Operational request logs expire under the hosting service’s configured rolling retention, unless a relevant record needs to be preserved for an investigation or legal obligation. You can ask the company contact for the period relevant to a specific request.

Saved Wallet preferences and account reader copies remain in account storage until replaced or removed through their respective controls, or handled through an applicable account or privacy request. Signing out or clearing browser data does not delete server-side account records. Removing either saved copy does not delete the Supabase Auth account or its linked base profile. For account deletion or questions about provider logs and backups, use the privacy contact below; the in-product delete controls do not promise immediate erasure of those separate records.

Correspondence is kept while needed to answer and follow up the request, and where necessary for a legal obligation or a documented dispute. Incident-related information is retained only as needed to investigate, protect the service and meet applicable obligations. Public source caches use their technical freshness limits; they are separate from personal correspondence.

Your choices and rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to legitimate-interest processing and withdraw consent for optional processing. Email office@dlkt.ee with enough context to locate the relevant processing, without sending passwords or unnecessary identity documents. We may need proportionate identity verification. We normally respond within one month of receiving a rights request. Where the law permits an extension, we explain it and the reasons within the initial month.

You may complain to a competent data-protection authority, including one in your country of residence. The Estonian authority is Andmekaitse Inspektsioon. You do not have to give up that route to contact us.

Personalisation and future features

The briefing uses chosen interests, city, source dates, availability and explicit browser feedback. DLKT does not make an automated decision about you with legal or similarly significant effects, such as deciding eligibility for credit, work or insurance.

Additional connection capabilities, advertising, paid products or materially different data uses require an updated notice and any additional choice or legal basis that applies. Their availability must not be inferred from account sign-in or private profile saving.
