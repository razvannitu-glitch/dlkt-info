What a source-linked update establishes, and what it does not.

General-interest coverage

The reader combines reviewed official feeds, licensed community reporting and source-checked news headlines across broad and specialist subjects. Global Voices and Electronic Frontier Foundation retain their named author, translation and licence credits where applicable. These are attributed reports, statements and perspectives; selection does not establish independent corroboration.

Coverage depends on the connected publishers and their availability. Sport, culture, business and other topic groups do not imply complete worldwide or neighbourhood coverage. Consumer prices, ticket availability and incident status require a relevant current source. Several feeds from one publisher do not become several independent confirmations.

Coverage and publisher claims

DLKT aggregates selected connected sources. It does not cover every publisher, city, event or development. A source check validates the supported source record and presentation rules; it is not independent corroboration of every statement in that record.

Publication dates, update dates, forecast periods and the time DLKT checked a source are different. A recent check does not make an older publication new. Scope and Next step describe the connected publisher and how to inspect its original record; they do not determine whether an item personally affects you. The selected country prioritises available reporting from its reviewed publisher editions within each category. A national publisher can report on events abroad; its country is not the location of the event. A local label requires separately supported city relevance. This is not a ranking of all world news.

Health, financial and legal decisions

General health, economic, legal or public-affairs information is not individual medical, investment, tax or legal advice. Regulatory announcements can have a particular country’s scope. Check the original document and an appropriately qualified professional where a decision requires one.

ECB figures are dated reference exchange rates, not executable prices or a live trading feed. Any percentage change shown by DLKT is calculated between the identified published references. No investment return or financial outcome is promised.

Weather, transport and events

Weather is a city-centre forecast for the stated period, not measured conditions at your address or an official emergency warning. Its illustration follows the current forecast-hour condition and day or night symbol supplied by the weather source. Unknown or expired conditions use a neutral illustration. Figures combined or converted by DLKT are labelled. Forecasts and operator notices can change.

A listed event is not a booking, a guarantee of admission, a verified ticket price or confirmation that a seat is available. Check the organiser. Missing or unavailable transport, weather or local coverage is not an all-clear. Use the relevant official emergency and travel channels when necessary.

Original sources and corrections

External sites have their own content, terms and privacy practices. Publisher names and marks identify sources and do not imply endorsement of DLKT. Third-party rights remain with their holders.
