## Current reading-card coverage — 22 November 2026

Every current top-level reading card receives a photograph through a three-step presentation lookup: its exact reviewed source photograph, a strictly matched reviewed context photograph, or a separately reviewed neutral editorial photograph. The final tier has its own `kind: editorial` discriminant and visible translated illustrative label. It depicts general reading, a globe or a digital workspace; it is not evidence about the article or a photograph of its event. This explicitly supersedes the earlier no-fallback presentation decision below while retaining all exact-source and subject-matching guards.

Their original credits, primary Commons pages and review windows are recorded in `src/media/editorial-photos.ts`. All source/context/editorial rights checks remain finite. Media affect presentation only, without altering source identities, hashes, freshness, story grouping or saved records. Article wording remains original. Image labels and the distinction between context and event photography have explicit copy in all30 supported interface locales.

Today, Explore, Saved, Daily, local information including forecasts, prepared-edition cards and public shared-card pages use the reviewed media path. Forecast animation remains separate and tied to its own verified period. Photographs use fixed dimensions, lazy loading, asynchronous decoding and same-origin URLs. When an image fails, its original title, source links and actions remain usable; no unreviewed external image is substituted. A missing or expired media review still fails closed.

Reader card colors follow the existing editorial category. Photographs fill a uniform 16:9 frame with proportional `cover` cropping, 16px outer spacing above and beside the image, and 10px corner radii. The complete title remains below in an opaque reading panel. Photo and category labels sit 12px inside the photo edges. Portrait, square and landscape originals use the same visible geometry, without letterboxing or stretching. Captions disclose the crop and retain the link to the complete original; source-photo collages are described as cropped details. Failed-image cards remove the photo spacing and retain their text and actions. Desktop hover is restrained, keyboard focus is visible, and movement follows reduced-motion preferences. No measured retention increase is claimed by these presentation changes.

## Relevant context photographs — 21 November 2026

The three exact reviewed article photographs retain priority. Context selection now requires an explicit matching headline subject or a public article path that identifies the sport or medium. A broad topic or source category alone is not sufficient; there is no universal image fallback. Unknown subjects keep the text and actions without an unrelated photograph. Cricket no longer inherits football, a film no longer inherits a concert, and military or crime stories do not inherit a library. Figurative weather expressions are excluded.

Sixteen additional independently credited Wikimedia Commons renditions are served locally with immutable SHA256 filenames, verified JPEG bytes and dimensions. The context catalog has 30 photographs. New subjects cover cricket, gaming, an explicitly dated Xi–Putin meeting, United Nations coverage, UK policy and economy, UK and Romanian housing, solar technology, cinema, euro monetary subjects, EU politics and US federal politics. Venue, country, currency and named-person photographs require the corresponding public subject; these are not illustrations for arbitrary articles in a broad category. Captions state the actual place and historical date where established. The image must not imply it depicts the currently reported event.

When a subject has multiple approved alternatives, a deterministic choice uses the canonical public article URL, with a title fallback for local presentation inputs. The same article receives the same photograph in the main reader, saved cards and shared recipient page. No personal preferences or private account data enter the choice. Failed image loads continue to remove only the photograph; useful card text and actions remain.

This is a finite reviewed catalog, not automatic publication of publisher Open Graph photographs. The original article image is still displayed only when its exact source and reuse conditions have been reviewed. No new runtime scraping service, database migration, external image hotlink, branding or CSS change is introduced by this correction.
