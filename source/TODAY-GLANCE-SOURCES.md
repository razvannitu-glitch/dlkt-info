DLKT INFO 0.9.19. Daily Life. Key Things.

Public purpose: a calendar date, explicit city and three optional daily signals. Browser preferences are not sent to an account or the public cache. No subscription, paid API, cron, trading or push delivery is activated. Intro and primary CTA are the user's approved brand direction; actual retention and organic distribution remain unmeasured.

ECB FX. Fixed original endpoint https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-hist-90d.xml was fetched with HTTP 200 and text/xml on 2026-11-15. Currency data are quoted per EUR; exact reference date is displayed. The previous published reference supplies the optional percentage change, calculated by DLKT, not an ECB-authored analysis. This is not a live or executable quote. The file is bounded to 1 MiB and eight seconds. Per-process refresh is coalesced, cached up to one hour, with a one-minute failure interval; public shared snapshots cache for five minutes. Serverless instances do not share a distributed quota. The seven-calendar-day reference-age limit and six-hour checked-age limit fail closed, including browser re-projection after expiry. Review expires after 2026-12-15.

ECB reuse: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/services/using-our-site/disclaimer/html/index.en.html permits accurate reproduction with attribution, requires modifications/calculations to be identified, and requires users to be told the source material is free when resold in a subscription service. The signal detail supplies attribution, original XML, the official human-readable page and the free-source notice. The project adds no paid-source commitment. ECB figures can be revised; the source's publication date is not replaced by the time DLKT fetched the feed.

Weather reuses the validated city-centre MET Norway snapshot already loaded by the local/daily controllers. It shows the minimum and maximum forecast temperatures for the original six-hour period, not a measured current temperature, rain probability, feels-like value or official warning. Details retain forecast period, issue/check times, boundary and MET Norway CC BY 4.0 attribution. Opening the page adds no duplicate header forecast request. Explicit Refresh glance requests the canonical displayed city directly, so a city restored from a saved personal briefing also refreshes correctly. Local transport notices appear only when an unexpired connected publisher notice exists, not as an all-clear.

Today's key thing first considers a still-upcoming, date-matched local event from existing reviewed municipal coverage. Otherwise it selects one recent record from NASA, USGS, US FDA or UK Government, within the existing reviewed source registry. US FDA and UK Government records enter the selection only for relevant country or explicitly chosen topic. Selected topics take precedence, then publication date and stable ID. The UI explicitly calls the fallback a selected publication, preserves publisher/date and explains selection. It is not a worldwide importance ranking, source corroboration or independent verification of the publisher's claims. Empty sources have no invented fallback. Existing source rights review/expiry controls remain in effect.

Optional BTC/ETH, Brent and stock indices were assessed independently against primary published terms on 2026-11-15. No inspected route was confirmed for commercial public redistribution without additional agreements. Coinbase market-data terms https://www.coinbase.com/legal/market_data and Kraken EEA terms https://www.kraken.com/legal/eea-terms do not automatically grant that use. EIA https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/prices.php republishes third-party prices under permission; its government-content reuse policy https://www.eia.gov/about/copyrights_reuse.php does not automatically cover those third-party series. S&P terms https://www.spglobal.com/en/terms-of-use and index disclaimers https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/disclaimers/ require relevant permission. These conclusions apply to the inspected sources, not every possible vendor. No optional quote widget is presented as connected. Nasdaq, FTSE and BET were not separately audited.

Verification contract: TODAY-GLANCE-ACCEPTANCE.md. Tests distinguish fixture parser/transport results from real-service observations. The release manifest and final delivery evidence identify the exact tested and deployed source. A screenshot proves visible UI, not persistence by itself. Device preferences require explicit Save; denied storage applies them for the visit and reports the limit. No promised traffic, virality, revenue or return rate is implied by this release.
